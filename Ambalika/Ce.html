DEPARTMENT OF CIVIL ENGINEERING

The department of Civil Engineering at AIMT takes pride in its longstanding commitment to engineering education and training. Since its establishment, it has been at the forefront of driving advancements in the field of engineering and technology. Our department has experienced remarkable growth over the years, making significant contributions to technology transfer and the dissemination of advancements in the industry. As the global technological landscape continues to evolve, we have kept pace by equipping ourselves with the latest state-of-the-art research and development facilities, advanced design and testing tools, modern instrumentation, and computational resources. One of our key strengths lies in our strong collaboration with the real estate and infrastructure industry. This partnership provides our students with a unique opportunity to bridge the gap between theory and practice. Through working modules and hands-on experiences, students get to apply their knowledge in the workforce.
