Welcome to Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology

COMPUTER SCIENCE COLLAGE - DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE & ENGINEERING

The department of Computer Science & Engineering at AIMT was established in 2008 and has made remarkable progress over the years. In today’s fast-paced world, computers serve as the driving force behind technological advancements, permeating every aspect of our lives. At AIMT, we recognize the profound impact that computing and technology have on society, shaping the way we live, communicate, and interact with our environment. We are committed to ensuring that our students and faculty members are equipped with the latest computing skills. Our primary focus is to empower our students with the knowledge and expertise required to excel in the digital arena.
