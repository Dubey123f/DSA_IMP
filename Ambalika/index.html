AMBALIKA INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT & TECHNOLOGY

The premier engineering college in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, Ambalika Group of Institutions, was founded to deliver high-quality professional education in the fields of engineering and management. Ambalika Construction Limited, Ambalika Chit Fund Limited, and Ambalika Farms & Resorts Ltd. are currently managed by the Trustee. The groups’ primary fields of activity are infrastructure development, building construction, machinery rental, transportation and logistics, earthwork, mining, and crushing. Ambalika Construction Limited, the group’s main firm, is in charge of the operation. Uttar Pradesh has abundant natural resources. However, it lags behind in technical and management education in particular. This has a negative impact on industrial growth and the economy as a whole. As a result, there is an urgent need for the state to expand management and engineering education.
